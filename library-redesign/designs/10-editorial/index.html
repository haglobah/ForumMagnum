Design note. Today's Library has no voice and buries its 271 community sequences behind “Load more” with no sign of which are good. This page reads like an issue of a magazine: one work featured and introduced by a librarian, an honest reading order for the core books, a short staff shelf, and a ranked board of community work with a score you can inspect. It trades breadth and browsing (no filters, no full catalogue on the page) for a point of view, and the featured work needs a human to choose and write it each season.

The Library

Sequences are LessWrong's books: posts written to be read in order, building one idea on another. Each season the librarians pick one to put on the desk, keep a short shelf of favourites, and let the community's own votes rank the rest.
