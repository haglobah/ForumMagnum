Design note. Today's Library knows what a signed-in reader has read, yet shows everyone the same five tiles and a catalogue. This design makes it a shelf you return to: where you left off, what is in progress, what you saved, what you finished, and what to read because of it. It trades away browsing and editorial showcase: the full catalogue is a plain list at the bottom, and a new reader is given one clear start rather than many.
