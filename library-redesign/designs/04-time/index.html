Design note

Today's Library never says how long anything is, though its works run from a 20-minute sequence to a 40-hour novel, so you can't tell what you could actually finish. This page starts from the one constraint every reader has, their time, and turns it into a concrete plan of real posts and minutes. It trades away browsing by topic, author and history: the catalogue only shows up as a length axis.
