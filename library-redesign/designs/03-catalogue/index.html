Design note. Today's Library is a wall of image tiles: no length, no topic, no date, no quality signal, no search, and 271 community sequences behind "Load more". The Catalogue treats the Library as a finder for people who know roughly what they want: every work in one dense list, filterable by topic, length, era, source and review winners, with each table of contents one keystroke away. It trades away the sense of a curated starting point and the pleasure of browsing pictures.
