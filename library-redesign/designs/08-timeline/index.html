Design note. Today's Library shows a sequence from 2008 and one from 2025 as identical tiles, so a reader can't tell a timeless foundation from a period piece, and the history of LessWrong's thinking is invisible. This design puts every sequence on one time axis, with the eras named, so when something was written becomes part of what it is. It trades away a clear "start here" and browsing by cover art: the timeline answers "what was this place thinking, and when?", not "what should I read first?".
