Design note. Today's Library shows its works as equal tiles in curation order, so nothing tells you that AI alignment is the largest part of it, that one novel is a fifth of the canon, or where the long works are. The Atlas draws the whole Library at once: area is reading time, depth of colour is median karma, gold marks Best of LessWrong winners, and you zoom from topic to work to single post. It trades away reading order and guidance: it shows the shape of the Library, not where to start. (The Highlights and Best of LessWrong are left out as works, since they repeat posts that are already on the map.)
