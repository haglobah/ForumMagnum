Design note. Today's Library shows every sequence as the same image tile, so a two-post sequence looks exactly like a thirty-hour book, and its subjects blur into one long grid. Here every work stands as a spine: thickness is reading time, colour and shelf are the topic, gold marks review winners, older bindings have faded. You see the size of a work and the shape of the library at a glance; the trade is the cover art (now inside the pulled-out book) and titles that run vertically and are slower to scan.
