Design note. Today's Library asks readers to commit hours to a sequence they can only judge by a cover image and a title in small caps. Here every sequence is introduced by its single strongest post (a Best of LessWrong winner where it has one, else its highest-karma post), so you can taste the writing first and see what the rest of the meal costs. It trades away the sequence as the unit you browse: covers, descriptions and chapter structure step back behind one post each.
