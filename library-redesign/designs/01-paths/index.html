Design note. Today's Library opens with five equal "Core Reading" tiles and a flat grid: no "start here", no "then what". This design replaces both with six curated reading paths drawn as a transit map, so a newcomer sees a first stop and every reader sees where each sequence leads. It trades away neutrality: the map is an editorial opinion about order, and sequences not on a line drop to a compact index below.

The Library

Six reading paths through LessWrong's long-form writing. Each stop is a sequence, sized in posts and hours; where lines cross, you can change. New here? Board at the Sequences Highlights.
